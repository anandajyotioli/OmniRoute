- **fix(sse):** a 429 whose typed Google `RetryInfo.retryDelay` is longer than the request-queue budget (Gemini answers an exhausted daily quota with `"86399s"`) no longer freezes the key's per-model rate limiter for the whole wait. #16528 already relays that window into the model lockout; the limiter still emptied its reservoir for the same window, so any call that reached the key before the window ended (after a resilience reset, a lockout cut short by the `maxCooldownMs` cap, or an alias the lockout did not name) sat out the full `maxWaitMs` and failed as a local 503. The wait now becomes a model lockout on that connection for the stated window (exact model on antigravity, plus the requested name when an alias resolved it) and the limiter stays unpaused. Prose and untyped hints and connection-wide limiters keep the pause, but a pause that outlasts a caller's remaining queue time now turns that call away at once instead of letting it wait the budget out, and the 429 header log no longer claims a pause it does not take ([#16525](https://github.com/diegosouzapw/OmniRoute/pull/16525)) — thanks @shipsfromrio
